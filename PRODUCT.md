# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Nairobi-based skincare and beauty customers, primarily browsing on mobile and desktop, who either shop skincare/wellness products online or book in-clinic dermatological treatments (facials, peels, consultations). They arrive wanting either quick, confident purchases (repeat skincare staples) or reassurance before booking a clinical treatment (new customers evaluating a real dermatology-adjacent service, not just a spa).

## Product Purpose

Premier Beauty Clinic is a combined ecommerce storefront and clinic booking site: customers shop dermatologist-informed skincare/wellness products and book in-clinic treatments (facials, peels, consultations, IV therapy) from the same site. Success is a purchase or a booking completed with confidence — the site has to read as credible enough to trust with skin health, not just another product catalog.

## Brand Personality

Elegant, warm, clinical-premium. Boutique-clinic feel: dermatological/professional credibility paired with warmth — never cold or sterile, never generic-storefront. Visual language already established: dark near-black chrome (#1A1A1A) with a purple accent (#6D4C91), serif-italic display headlines against sans-serif body text, light-purple page backgrounds (#F2F1F8).

## Anti-references

- Generic ecommerce/Shopify-template look: interchangeable card grids, default icon sets, no point of view.
- Cold clinical/medical-device UI: sterile hospital-software feel that undercuts the warmth half of the brand.

## Design Principles

- Credibility first: every UI decision should read as "a real clinic," not "a dropshipping storefront" — restraint and precision over decoration.
- Warmth without softness: elegant and inviting, but not fussy, pastel-cute, or spa-generic.
- One accent, used deliberately: the purple (#6D4C91) marks action and brand moments; it doesn't flood the UI.
- Dual-intent navigation: shopping and booking are equally first-class journeys, never one buried under the other.
- Never converge on generic AI-storefront patterns — every surface should feel deliberately art-directed for this specific clinic.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum contrast across text/icons on both the dark navbar and light page backgrounds. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for menu/drawer/carousel animations. Full keyboard and focus support on nav, dropdowns, search, and mega-menu.
